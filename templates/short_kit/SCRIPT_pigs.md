# Pigs short: SCRIPT (v1, approved by the user 2026-11-08)

## Facts + sources
- **Megara, c. 266 BC.** King Antigonus II Gonatas besieged Megara with war elephants. The Megarians smeared pigs with pitch, set them alight and drove them at the elephants. The elephants, maddened by the squealing burning pigs, broke ranks and fled. Antigonus then ordered his Indian mahouts to rear pigs among the elephants, so they would get used to the sight and sound of them.
  - Sources: Aelian, *On Animals* 16.36; Polyaenus, *Stratagems* 4.6.3. Both were written ~300+ years after the siege (late sources). Summary: [War pig, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_pig); [Bad Ancient: incendiary pigs](https://www.badancient.com/claims/incendiary-pigs/).
- **Elephants and the pig's squeal.** Pliny, *Natural History* 8.9 (elephants are frightened by the smallest squeal of a pig). Aelian 16.36 suggests the squeal as the cause. The script says "ancient writers swore", so it doesn't claim this as modern fact.
- **Edessa, 544 AD.** Khosrow I's Persians besieged Edessa and pushed an elephant up to the wall. The Roman defenders hung a pig from the tower; its squealing frightened the elephant, which retreated.
  - Sources: Procopius, *History of the Wars* 8.14.35-37 (Suda entry quoting Procopius); [Siege of Edessa (544), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Edessa_(544)).
- **Time gap.** 266 BC to 544 AD is ~810 years, so "eight hundred years later" holds.
- **"Crash back through their own army":** the routed elephants fled back through Antigonus' own lines. The sources say they were thrown into disorder and bolted. Trampling is the standard reading of a stampede, so the script says "crash back through", not "killed".

## Script (template v2: 150 words, longest sentence 10 words)
Title: The Burning Pigs That Beat an Army of Elephants

Script:
Nobody could stop war elephants until pigs started screaming.
Greece, 266 BC. King Antigonus marches his elephants on Megara.
Spears bounce off their hides. Arrows barely sting them.
The elephants smash through the city's last defenders.
So the Megarians raid their own pig pens.
They smear the pigs with sticky black pitch.
Then they set every one on fire.
The gates swing open, and burning pigs charge out shrieking.
The elephants freeze in their tracks.
Then they spin around and stampede in terror.
They crash straight back through their own army.
Ancient writers swore elephants panic at a pig's squeal.
So Antigonus ordered his trainers to raise pigs with elephants.
Every elephant grew up hearing that squeal daily.
Eight hundred years later, Persia pushed an elephant against Edessa.
The Romans didn't light anything this time.
They just hung one pig from the wall.
It squealed, and the giant elephant ran.

3. Ending: the Edessa tower, one pig dangling on a rope in front of the elephant's eye, then the elephant turning tail; the framing matches frame 0 so it loops.
