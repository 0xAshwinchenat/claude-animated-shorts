## Picture: what the newest two look like
- World: stylised-PBR game-engine look. Detailed assets (cars with full interiors, leather seats with stitching,
  gauges, glass with reflections; coaster track with metal specular; houses, pools, string lights). Real materials:
  specular, clearcoat on car paint, wet road reflections, glass. Ambient occlusion in creases. Hard crisp sun shadows.
  Dense foliage, grass as individual blades. Sky: saturated deep blue, stylised cumulus clouds. Distance fades into
  a BLUISH aerial haze (not a warm veil). No ink lines on the world any more (cars, roads, houses have none);
  the ink outline is on the characters only.
- VFX: volumetric smoke puffs and explosions (shaded, with a shockwave ring), dust trails, rigid-body crash debris
  (bits tumble), glowing light-ribbon trails on fast objects, rain, hundreds of instanced small props (cash bundles).
- Motion blur: smooth directional streaks only on fast motion (road texture during a pan); tracked subjects stay sharp.
  NO stepped copies. Ours shows 3-4 stepped copies on every fast mover (tmix of 4 sub-frames at a 360 deg shutter).
- Transitions: crossfades between places (as before), continuous camera inside a place, POV cams (driver seat,
  dashcam, coaster car).

- Ours (an/ours_chars.jpg): small thin features, fewer expressions, plain flat costumes, soft/blurry heads, ghosting.
