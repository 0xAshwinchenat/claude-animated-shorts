# Asset kit licenses

Everything here was downloaded on **2026-11-09**. **623 of the 631 models are CC0 1.0**, and so are all textures and HDRIs.
**8 models are CC-BY 3.0.** They need a credit line in the video description whenever they appear on screen. They are listed under "CC-BY 3.0 models" below. Each one is also flagged `"license": "CC-BY-3.0"` in `MANIFEST.json` and marked "CC-BY" on the contact sheets.

How each pack was sourced:
- Original archives and files are kept in `D:\youtube\assetkit_src\<pack>\` and are not shipped.
- The build scripts are in `D:\youtube\assetkit_src\_tools\` (`kit.config.mjs`, `build.mjs`, `tex.mjs`).
- Models were merged into family GLBs, rescaled to metres and deduplicated.
- Textures were re-encoded at a maximum of 1024 px (JPG, or PNG for alpha and small gradient atlases).
- Two recoloured variants were made. These modifications are allowed under CC0 and CC-BY.
